Restore purchases

Bought Remove Ads or Idle Cap 12h and they’re missing after reinstall or a new device?

  1. Open Windward while signed into the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.
  2. Use the in-app Restore Purchases button (usually in Settings / Shop).
  3. If restore fails, email teabonetv@gmail.com with your store platform and approximate purchase date — we can guide you, but refunds and receipt ownership are controlled by Apple/Google.

Purchases are non-consumable (buy once per store account).

Common FAQ

How long does offline progress run?

  • Default: up to 6 hours of offline idle progress.
  • With the Idle Cap 12h IAP: up to 12 hours.

Progress beyond the cap does not accumulate until you open the app and claim again.

Do live prizes work offline?

No. Live prizes / live events do not run while you are offline. Open the app to participate when they are live.

When do ads appear?

Only at claim time (when you claim idle/offline rewards) — not mid-fight, and not on a fixed 15-minute timer. Buy Remove Ads to remove them.

Is there a login?

No required login. Progress is kept on your device. Uninstalling may wipe local progress; store purchases can still be restored.

Are there loot boxes or gacha?

No. No loot boxes, gacha, or power MTX. Optional IAPs are Remove Ads and Idle Cap 12h only.
