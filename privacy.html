This Privacy Policy explains how Windward (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the App on iOS or Android. We aim to collect only what is needed to run the game, process purchases, show ads (where applicable), and fix crashes.

Windward is published by Luke Crafer as an individual in the UK. We are not a limited company.

1. Who this policy is for

Windward is rated for ages 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, contact teabonetv@gmail.com and we will delete it where reasonably possible.

You do not need an account or login to play.

2. What information may be collected

Depending on your device, store, and consent choices, the App (and its service providers) may process:

2.1 Device and app information

  • Device model, OS version, language/region settings
  • App version and basic performance data
  • Approximate diagnostics needed to keep the App running

2.2 Purchase / IAP receipts

  • Non-personal purchase tokens and receipt validation data via Apple App Store and/or Google Play when you buy:
    • Remove Ads (non-consumable)
    • Idle Cap 12h (non-consumable; extends offline progress from 6 hours to 12 hours)

We do not receive your full payment card details; those stay with Apple or Google.

2.3 Advertising identifiers (if ads are shown)

If you have not purchased Remove Ads, the App may show claim-time interstitial ads only (when you claim idle/offline rewards — not mid-fight and not on a fixed 15-minute timer).

Ad networks may use advertising IDs (e.g. IDFA on iOS subject to ATT; GAID on Android) and similar technical data, subject to your consent where required (ATT on iOS; UMP / Google consent on Android where applicable).

Ad network(s): Google AdMob

2.4 Analytics (if used)

Aggregated or pseudonymous usage events (e.g. session length, feature usage) to improve the game.

Analytics provider(s): None

2.5 Crash and error logs

Crash reports and stack traces (e.g. via a crash reporter) to diagnose bugs.

Crash reporting: None

2.6 What we do not do

  • No required login or account creation
  • No loot boxes, gacha, or pay-to-win power microtransactions
  • Live prizes / live events do not progress while you are offline
  • We do not sell your personal data for money

4. Ads, ATT, and UMP

  • Ads: Claim-time interstitials only. Purchase Remove Ads to stop them.

  • Android / Google UMP (or equivalent): Where required, we show a consent form before using non-essential ad/analytics cookies or identifiers.
  • Exact behaviour depends on your region, store rules, and the SDKs listed as Google AdMob (analytics/crash: None).

5. Sharing

We may share technical data with:

  • Apple / Google — store, billing, and platform services
  • Google AdMob — advertising (if ads are shown)
  • Analytics and crash reporting: None at launch (no analytics or crash SDK configured)

These providers process data under their own policies and our configuration. We do not sell personal data for money.

6. International transfers

Providers may process data outside the UK/EEA (e.g. US). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards used by those platforms (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms offered by Apple, Google, or the SDK vendor).

7. Retention and deletion

  • On-device game progress is stored locally on your device; uninstalling typically removes it (unless cloud save is later added — currently no required login / no account cloud save).
  • Purchase entitlements are retained by Apple/Google and can be restored via the store restore flow.
  • Ad/analytics/crash data is retained according to each provider’s retention settings (often aggregated or short-lived).
  • To request deletion of any personal data we hold that we can identify, email teabonetv@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time (generally within 30 days under UK GDPR rights).
